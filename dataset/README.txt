Stackwise dataset: Rental Housing Law Navigator (Hack-Nation x RealPage, Challenge 2)
Not legal advice. Public sources only. Built from a public corpus that no lawyer has reviewed.

input/
  sample_addresses.csv   The 500 sample addresses supplied by the organizers (public parcel data).
                         Columns: address_id, street_address, postal_city, state, zip, year_built, units, use_code,
                         use_description, source_dataset, retrieved_at
  corpus_manifest.csv    The list of public source pages (doc_id, jurisdictions, url, source_type, capture,
                         retrieved_at, sha256, text_file, status). The text of pages we were allowed to store
                         is in ../corpus/text/. Pages whose terms do not clearly allow copying are listed by URL only.

output/   (generated by `python src/run_all.py`, as of the query date 2026-10-01)
  rules.json     56 rules. One record per rule: team_rule_id, jurisdiction, level (state|city), category, status
                 (in_force|not_yet_effective|pending|failed), title, requirement, key_value, coverage_conditions,
                 exemptions, overrides (rule ids this rule gives way to), interaction, effective_date, citation,
                 source_doc_id, source_url, quoted_span (exact words from the source), confidence, conflict_flag,
                 conflict_note, retrieved_at, supporting_sources.
  lookups.json   {"as_of": "2026-10-01", "lookups": {address_id: [ {team_rule_id, result, explanation,
                 conflict_flag}, ... ]}}. 500 addresses, 4,773 answers. result is one of: applies, superseded,
                 not_yet_effective, pending, unknown.
  changes.json   Tests T1 to T5: {"T1": {"affected_address_ids": [...], "notes": "..."}, ...}. T3 also has
                 conflict_flag_address_ids.

More detail, intermediate files and logs are in ../outputs/. Schema: ../schema/rule_record.schema.json.
